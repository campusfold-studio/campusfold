CAMPUSFOLD · Proposed student laundry collection coordination platform

CampusFold is a student prototype. The booking and enquiry forms only preview your details in the browser: nothing is sent or stored, and the site sets no cookies. A live service would collect only the data needed to arrange a collection, publish a privacy notice under UK GDPR and ask consent before any non-essential cookies (ICO, 2026).
